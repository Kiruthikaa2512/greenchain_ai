## 🟢 Overview
**GreenChain AI** is a unified sustainability intelligence system designed to help organizations measure and mitigate environmental impact across global supply chains. While modern supply chains generate the majority of corporate emissions, data is often scattered and unactionable. 

GreenChain AI solves this by unifying transportation, forecasting, supplier ESG, and warehouse data into a single intelligence layer. Powered by **Gemma 4**, the platform provides **Grounded Reasoning** to explain how operational decisions influence long-term resilience and carbon outcomes.

---

## 🛠️ What the Project Does
GreenChain AI integrates four core analytical modules with a central **Gemma 4 Reasoning Engine**.

### 1. Transportation & Routing Intelligence
* **The Problem:** Inefficient routing and low load utilization drive unnecessary Scope 1 emissions.
* **The Solution:** Quantifies the impact of vehicle transitions (Diesel to Electric) and optimizes load factors.
* **Gemma's Role:** Analyzes carbon mitigation strategies and provides executive summaries on fleet optimization.

### 2. Demand & Waste Forecasting
* **The Problem:** Overproduction creates "Sunk Carbon"—resources wasted on goods that never reach consumers.
* **The Solution:** Uses linear regression to identify overstock risks and resource loss patterns.
* **Gemma's Role:** Acts as an "Inventory Auditor," suggesting safety-stock adjustments to balance service levels with waste reduction.

### 3. Supplier Sustainability (ESG) Audit
* **The Problem:** Scope 3 emissions and supplier ethics are notoriously difficult to quantify.
* **The Solution:** A weighted multi-criteria system evaluates suppliers on ESG maturity and transparency.
* **Gemma's Role:** Interprets complex supplier data to identify opportunities for more resilient and ethical sourcing.

### 4. Warehouse Energy & Ops
* **The Problem:** Warehouses are energy hubs, especially regarding refrigeration and heavy equipment.
* **The Solution:** Models daily kWh usage and simulates optimization scenarios.
* **Gemma's Role:** Suggests operational shifts to lower energy intensity and operational costs.

## 🛡️ Intelligence, Safety, & Trust

### **The Gemma Audit Trace**
In high-stakes logistics, "Black Box" AI is a liability. GreenChain AI implements a **Gemma Audit Trace**—a transparent reasoning layer that links every executive recommendation directly back to evaluated operational metrics. This ensures the AI’s advice is:
* **Grounded:** Based on real uploaded data.
* **Traceable:** Transparent logic for human oversight.
* **Actionable:** Directly linked to operational KPIs.

### **Unified Sustainability Index**
The platform aggregates all scores into a holistic 0–100 metric:
* **Transportation (30%)** | **Waste (25%)** | **Supplier ESG (25%)** | **Warehouse (20%)**

## 💻 Technical Implementation
* **Frontend:** Streamlit (Python)
* **Intelligence Layer:** Gemma 4 (31B) via Google GenAI API
* **Data Science:** Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn
* **Visualization:** Plotly (Interactive Radar & Trend Charts)
* **Reporting:** ReportLab (Enterprise-ready PDF Sustainability Audits)

**Digital Sovereignty:** The architecture is "Edge-Ready," designed to support local deployment via **Ollama** or **LiteRT** for private, air-gapped enterprise environments.
